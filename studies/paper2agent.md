### Reported results and limitations
From 100 biology papers, 74 could be agentified, yielding 599 proposed tools, 593 validation passes. Paper-backed MCP tools bind to reference code/results and are tested; not all research repos runnable. Benchmarks stress reuse/faithfulness; high-level scientific interpretation still human-owned.

### DSH-specific research disposition
**ACCEPT a bounded experiment** using 1–3 well documented research codebases to build validated *sandboxed* MCP adapters, compare with manual wrapping, and preserve references/tests/hashes; **reject automatic trust/public deployment**. Not a general new agent framework.
