## Method, results and caveats

Trajectory-aware placement of adversarial text or fake dialogs on Android application pages. Automated testing records baseline mobile-agent trajectory, chooses an injection point, synthesizes context-aware in-app content and executes the mobile agent under controlled Android virtual devices; test oracle checks observable final-state misuse. Authors test **83 instances across 12 mobile applications**, three mobile GUI agents; on selected configurations ASR reaches ~85% in paper, not field rate. Injection Point Coverage deliberately measures whether a selected step was reached, distinguishing attack effectiveness from exposure; authors report low but nonzero miss rate. Authors openly list omissions of finance and healthcare domains, per-instance handcrafted attack goals, high cost of actual evaluations and irreproducibility of changed GUI paths. These constraints dominate transfer.
